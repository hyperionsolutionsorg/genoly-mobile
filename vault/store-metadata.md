## Long description (both stores)

Genoly is your family's daily home — the living version of your family tree.

**Your family, every day**
- See today's birthdays and anniversaries the moment you open the app
- Keep your contribution and visit streaks alive 🔥👋
- A daily family game pick, from Family Wordle to Timeline Tap

**Walk together — Family Walking Challenges**
- Pool steps with your cousins toward a team goal ("Walk to the moon — 386,000 steps!")
- Or race head-to-head on a live family leaderboard
- Steps sync privately from Apple Health / Health Connect — only for challenges you join, and you can leave or hide your activity anytime

**Grow the tree from your pocket**
- Add relatives, life events, and photos straight from your camera
- Browse profiles, explore relationships, search the whole family

**Built for every generation**
- Large, readable type and three themes — including a classic heirloom look
- Private by design: your tree is yours; per-family isolation, no ads, no tracking SDKs

Genoly is free on mobile. Genoly is a product of Hyperion Solutions LLC, an Illinois limited liability company. Privacy: https://genoly.org/privacy
